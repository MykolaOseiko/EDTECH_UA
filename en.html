Everything you need to know before the conference

I have paid for my ticket. What happens next?+

Instructions for ticket holders will be published when registration opens.

What language is the conference in?+

Ukrainian.

Will the talks be recorded?+

No recordings are planned. We want to emphasise the value of live participation and engagement.

Is catering included?+

Yes. In-person tickets include welcome coffee and a full buffet coffee break.

Can I get a ticket refund?+

If unforeseen circumstances arise, we offer:

  • A 100% refund 30 days before the event.
  • A 50% refund 14 days before the event.
  • A 10% refund 3 working days before the event.

If the event is cancelled, we offer a 100% refund.

How can I switch between online and in-person tickets?+

Instructions for switching ticket types will be available when registration opens.

How do I get to the venue?+

The event will take place in Kyiv. The venue and directions will be shared closer to the conference.

Is parking available at the venue?+

Parking arrangements are being confirmed. Details will be added closer to the event.
